# Product Vision

## Product Name

Customer Portal

---

# Vision

Customer Portal is a web application that allows customers to register accounts, authenticate, manage personal information, and interact with services provided by the company.

The platform serves as a foundational customer management system that may later evolve into a broader CRM and self-service portal.

---

# Business Goal

Provide customers with a secure and convenient way to:

- create accounts;
- authenticate;
- manage personal information;
- access customer services.

---

# Target Users

Primary users:

- Customers

Future users:

- Customer Support Representatives
- Administrators

---

# Success Criteria

The platform should allow:

- customer self-registration;
- customer authentication;
- profile management;
- secure storage of credentials;
- role-based access control.

---

# MVP Scope

The initial MVP focuses on:

- Registration
- Login
- Customer Profile
- Basic Security

---

# Out of Scope

The following items are intentionally excluded from MVP:

- Multi-factor authentication
- Social login
- Password recovery
- Customer Support Portal
- Billing
- Notifications
- Document management
