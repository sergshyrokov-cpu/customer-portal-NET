# Business Glossary

## Customer

A person who owns an account in the Customer Portal.

---

## Account

Technical representation of customer access credentials.

---

## Registration

Process of creating a new customer account.

---

## Authentication

Process of verifying customer identity.

---

## Authorization

Process of determining what actions a customer may perform.

---

## Profile

Collection of customer personal information.

---

## Administrator

Internal user responsible for system administration.

---

## Role

Permission group assigned to a user.

Possible values:

- CUSTOMER
- ADMIN
